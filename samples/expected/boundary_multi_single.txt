Main memory size (KB): How many jobs?   Name of job 1 (e.g. J1):   Memory needed by J1 (KB):   CPU burst of J1 (ticks):   Arrival time of J1 (ticks): Time quantum for the CPU (ticks): 
Simulation rules:
  - first-fit contiguous loading, memory = 10 KB
  - Round Robin on the CPU, quantum = 1 tick(s)
  - each job does ONE I/O of 3 ticks at the halfway point of its burst, one job at a time on the device
  - a job only exists for the OS once its arrival time is reached

Tick | CPU | READY (queue order) | WAITING | I/O device | JOB QUEUE | not arrived | memory
-----+-----+---------------------+---------+------------+-----------+-------------+-------
0    | CPU: J1 (0 left) | READY: - | WAITING: - | I/O device: free | QUEUE: - | not arrived: - | MEM: - | J1 arrived -> JOB QUEUE; J1 LOADED (10 KB at 0); J1 -> RUNNING; J1 FINISHED (memory freed)
    memory: [free 0-10] | free total 10 KB, largest hole 10 KB

All jobs finished at tick 1.

------ -------- -------- ------ ---------- ------------ ----------- --------
J1            0       10      1          0            1           1        0

Total simulation time  : 1 ticks

Note: turnaround = finished at - arrival time. Waiting time counts
the ticks spent in the job queue, the ready queue or the I/O queue;
time running and time actually on the I/O device are not waiting.
